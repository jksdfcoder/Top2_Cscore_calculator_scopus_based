# Interface decisions

This is an Operate surface: a calm, minimal single page whose first screen is the whole task. Three numbered steps (add exports, confirm author, calculate) sit beside the score panel. On a desktop viewport of 1280×720 or larger, the import, identity, action and both scores are visible without scrolling. On narrow screens the steps stack above the score, and calculating scrolls the score into view.

Help lives where it is needed, as dialogs: "How to export three files from Scopus" sits in step 1 (steps visible, early-window and privacy notes folded), and "Methodology" sits in the score panel footer. Below the fold there are at most two chapters: data checks (only when there are issues), then one "Score and standing" module. Its Career / Single year tabs drive both halves. The radar labels axes with indicator values; You is gold, the compared listed author a dashed green outline, named in a fixed three-line legend that never changes height. The standing bar is the subfield rank on a log scale, rank 1 at the left: green is the official top 2% of ranks, white is everyone after, gold marks You (interpolated between listed authors, or between published percentiles with self-citations). Leader lines join You and Top 2% to their labels. A block in the bar picks a listed author; drag gain follows pointer speed, and a slow drag shows a readout and fine rank labels near the block. The empty state keeps the bar's height so switching field does not shift the page. Chapters fade and rise in with native CSS scroll-driven animation (`animation-timeline: view()`), gated by `@supports` and `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`. Content is fully visible without the animation. Do not add a scroll library. After each calculation the score panel plays one short sequence: formula, six contribution segments (each its share of the maximum 6), then the number; the top 2% band grows from rank 1 when a subfield is chosen. Toggling self-citations does not replay it, and reduced motion removes it.

Palette: light warm gray content column (`#f3f3f0`) on a slightly deeper page edge (`#e8e8e3`), near-white panels, deep forest green (`forest-700 #1f4d3c`) for primary actions and score numerals, and deep gold (`gold-700 #735423`) for the headline accent and secondary actions. These are Nuxt UI custom colors (`primary: forest`, `secondary: gold`, `neutral: stone`). Gold is never used for status alone, and lighter gold appears only on non-text chart fill. Text meets WCAG AA on the gray ground. Inline links inside text are underlined.

Use Nuxt UI controls and the system sans stack. Scores use tabular numerals: 3 decimals in the hero, 6 in the breakdown table. Only the score panel is a card. Do not add a sidebar, gradients, decorative cards or icons beside headings. Graphical data always has an equivalent table. Warnings name the missing input and the next action.
