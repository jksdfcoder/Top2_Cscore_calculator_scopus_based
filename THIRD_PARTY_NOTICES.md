`public/reference/v9/maxlog.json` transcribes logarithmic normalization denominators from official Table 3 workbooks, attributed to John P. A. Ioannidis, August 2026 data-update, Elsevier Data Repository V9, DOI [10.17632/btchxktzyw.9](https://doi.org/10.17632/btchxktzyw.9). This data is CC BY-NC 3.0, not MIT. See the adjacent LICENSE.txt. The application does not bundle official researcher-level cohorts yet.

Fixtures in tests/fixtures/public and public/demo are synthetic examples created for this project. No personal Scopus exports or authenticated account screenshots are distributed.
